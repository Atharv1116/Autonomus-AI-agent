Write your analysis in clean Markdown format with the following structure:

### 💼 Business Implications
- What these findings mean for decision-making
- Impact on revenue, costs, or operations

### 📈 Trends & Patterns
- Notable trends observed in the data

### ⚠️ Anomalies & Outliers

### ✅ Recommendations
- Actionable steps based on the analysis

### 🔮 Future Analysis
- Follow-up questions to explore
